# Independent witness and suite map (private acceptance material)

Authority: PUBLIC_SPEC v1.2 (D1/D2) and SOURCE_REQUIREMENTS R1–R9. No EXPERT_PLAN or implementation-derived expected outputs were used. Source transcription, not a fresh visual PDF comparison, is the numerical source for this suite.

## Small witness, independently derived

The fixture is intentionally **not** a plan to meet all six deadlines. It demonstrates that mandatory work, actual continuous validation production and task/identity coverage can coexist. Deadline and unfinished-campaign quota warnings are acceptable; physical errors are not.

- A REWIND-1 runs [0,90): 2500 × .85 / 1000 × 90 = **191.25**, below its 200 initial input. GRIND-1 runs [0,280): .68 × 280 = **190.4**. Thus the input before GRIND closes at **.85**, an internal stock not represented by a stage total.
- A WASH/CHROME/TEF run [0,240). WASH has 250 initial units plus continuous .68 GRIND inflow. Even when its initial stock empties, its rate remains positive. CHROME is limited to 2.278 and later upstream supply; TEF may throttle but never stops. VAL-1 therefore has actual A production for all 240 minutes, with its own extra worker. No validation quarantine/yield is assumed.
- B PRESS-4 → THERMAL-3 → PRINT-2 supplies .765, not .765 × .85 at every edge. REWIND-2 additionally has 100 initial units. Two 180-minute B GRIND validations consume **244.8** combined, below 100 + .765 × 420 = **421.3** available by their end. The staggered choice uses GRIND-4 [0,180), GRIND-5 [240,420); parallel uses both [240,420). There is no B CHROME edge.
- C PRESS-5 and THERMAL-2 finish their respective 25/30-minute setups at 1260. PRESS → THERMAL → LASER run [1260,1500), all at the PRESS-limited .765. LASER output is **183.6** and VAL-3 is continuous. No C PRINT edge exists.
- Three A WRAP validations run [1260,1440). Each produces .24225 × 180 = **43.605**, total **130.815**, from the initial 300 WRAP input. PACK-1 can consume the simultaneous .72675 flow; it does not consume stage stock directly. Closing WRAP input is **169.185**. All this finished output is allocated late to ORD-1, leaving its original S1 deficit **1400** visible.
- Every maintenance record has the exact duration/window: PRESS-1 [420,600), GRIND-2 [420,660), THERMAL-1 [420,600), CHROME-2 [0,240), PACK-2 [1260,1440). GRIND maintenance legally crosses a shift boundary. None overlaps production on its machine.
- Hand-specified task windows generate separate worker IDs within each shift, not evaluator-selected staff. Required peaks are **[13,2,10,0]** staggered and **[11,2,10,0]** parallel. Required labor minutes are **[3970,55,2040,0]**, total **6065**, for either choice. Fixed requests/forecasts of 40 are deliberately generous, explicitly justified reserves, not an asserted optimum.
- The repair removes [120,270): .68 × 150 = **102** from GRIND-1. Its existing normal worker can cover A GRIND-3 instead during that interval. The transfer has quota 102; material is sufficient because total A GRIND output stays 190.4. It is neither maintenance nor validation. Initial stock is never reinjected. After the two-person absence, 38 S2 workers remain and this witness's two setup tasks still fit.

These are hand calculations, not executed feasibility claims while the evaluator is a throw stub. The suite requires the future real evaluator to prove every relevant machine/queue/staff/job row. A counterfactual projection with staffing errors cannot be agreed.

## Suite map

- `unit/source.test.ts`: exact independent constants, all routes/settings, immutable nested fixtures.
- `unit/calendar.test.ts`: four shifts, every exclusion plateau, handovers, fractional conversion/integration, range errors.
- `unit/engine.test.ts`: valid mandatory witness; full routes for all products; zero-buffer simultaneous flow; fractional quota exhaustion/sharing; stock carry; setup/stop union; work counts/windows/production; grouped versus separate staff tasks and identities; PACK allocation, original/promised deficits, comparisons; malformed input and event errors.
- `unit/workflow.test.ts`: real evaluation, roles, noon/fixed requests, warning acknowledgements, paired approvals, immutable predecessors, failure then absence, frozen prefixes and legal cut split, D1-specific guards, custom orders/promises, deterministic export and restoration.
- `unit/scenario.test.ts`: public D2 factory/template contracts, both shipped alternatives and full real agreement lineage, source/input independence, computed-budget peak/reserve bounds, phase refusals, physical idempotence, preserved unrelated work/orders/promises/null or fixed requests, and no duplicate schedule records. Headcount bounds and reserve evidence are derived from the report; no exact private reserve-policy formula or expert counts are imposed.
- `unit/contract-edges.test.ts`: public sequential setup/conflict/union/fractional boundaries; malformed records and operations; invalid compare sides; workflow guard and quota-prefix edges; exact D2 finite-JSON diagnostic export, including otherwise complete workflows with one invalid public field.
- `support/execution.ts` and `unit/execution-witness.test.ts`: EPS-aware partition-independent comparison of machine/work/settings/staff/material prefixes and full outputs; six valid/corrupted isolated witness checks. Floating integration is compared numerically, never with exact floating-point array equality.
- `support/ledger.ts`: independent conservation witness checker shared by domain/browser tests, with machine horizons, rates, campaign quotas, every actual queue edge, shift snapshots, task-specific staffing/identities, labor/reserve and allocation conservation.
- `support/setup-witness.ts`: unconditionally invoked by common checkLedger. It reconstructs configuration from independent R6/R4 initial-setting/matrix data and valid completed chronological setups, rather than trusting a campaign/report product pair. Wrong/conflicting/stopped attempts cannot commit new products. `unit/setup-witness.test.ts` supplies 19 hand-built valid/corrupted setup-only checks, including honest invalid-report behavior, not evaluator mocks or factory feasibility claims.
- `support/work-witness.ts`: unconditionally called by the ledger checker; independently reconstructs mandatory work counts/windows/durations, actual maintenance stops and continuous named-product validation with normal/extra worker identities, then checks every JobResult. No feasible flag is used as work evidence. `unit/work-witness.test.ts` checks the checker itself with valid and corrupted hand-built job witnesses, never an evaluator mock.
- `e2e/simulation.test.ts`: four full-lineage cases (390/1440 × light/dark), custom/invalid/safe-text/promise/reset case and roles/keyboard/theme case. Existing Playwright configuration repeats each case three times with zero retries. Local axe is invoked for planner/manager/failure/absence/restored states; all findings are attached, serious/critical fail. Auto fixture blocks and records remote HTTP/WebSockets and console/page errors.
- `e2e/display.ts`: comparison figures are checked against each separately captured alternative export, with selected-template rationale; passport revision/phase, shift/order/job/staff figures are checked against the current export at each event/edit/agreement and for custom promises. Named tables are scoped semantically, not by CSS. `reporters/` provides bounded raw JSON diagnostics without changing runner status or test configuration.

## Review boundaries

The public semantic UI names and three-decimal numeric display rule are stated separately in PUBLIC_UI_CONTRACT.md for orchestrator acceptance. Browser cases depend on that agreement, not incidental DOM/CSS. Exact named template placements follow the current public candidate table; an infeasible candidate must be disclosed/revised through an accepted public amendment, not silently relabelled feasible or worked around by weaker assertions. Display checks allow .000501 absolute rounding error (integer counts retain exactness); domain arithmetic uses 1e-6, not display precision. Comparison codes may share their column cell with a Russian label; other IDs remain exact. Invalid modal input is cancelled normally before background export.

This initial suite does not establish final branch coverage, full accessibility, PDF printing fidelity, slide narrative/pilot metrics quality, or universal optimization/deadline feasibility. Additional independent tests may be required after primary/secondary review and once real control-flow/contract gaps are known. No skip, todo, xfail, evaluator mock or acceptance assertion that all orders finish on time is used.
